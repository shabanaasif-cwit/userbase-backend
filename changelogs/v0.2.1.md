### Fixed

- `GET /api/reminders` `read=true` / `read=false` query now applies to **admin** callers as well as regular users. Admins can filter to reminders where they are a **recipient** and their row is read or unread. Reminders that do not include the current user in `recipients` are omitted when `read` is set, so “read only” / “unread only” no longer behave like a no-op for admins.
