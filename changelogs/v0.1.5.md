### Changed

- Reminder recipient resolution now accepts only notification `targetType` values `users`, `role`, and `all` (removed separate `user` and `admin` branches).

- Default `FRONTEND_ORIGIN` in runtime config, `.env.example`, and tests is `http://localhost:3000` (replacing `http://localhost:5173`).
